Knockout Cup

Two leagues of 14. One gameweek settles each round, right through to the Final on GW38.

League setup

Find it in the league's URL: fantasy.premierleague.com/leagues/<ID>/...
Check the URL — "/standings/c" is classic, "/matches/h" is head-to-head.

Note: your combined 28-manager classic league (1307897) isn't used here — this bracket runs independently within each group of 14.

Format: Qualifying (GW1–3 combined points) seeds all 14 entries 1–14. Seeds 1 & 2 receive a bye into the Quarter-Final; seeds 3–14 are drawn in a standard bracket (3v14, 4v13, 5v12, 6v11, 7v10, 8v9). Round of 14 is decided on GW11, Quarter-Final on GW19, Semi-Final on GW27, and the Final on GW38 — each round a single gameweek's score. Ties are broken by qualifying seed.
